Our approach

No fad diets. Just real food and real results.

Good nutrition is not about restriction. It is about understanding what your body needs and building habits you can enjoy for life.

Our nutritionists design plans around the foods you already know and love — from ugali and sukuma wiki to fresh Seychellois fish and fruit. Because mental and physical health are connected, we also look at how food affects your mood, energy, sleep and stress.

  • Personalised assessment of your diet, health and lifestyle
  • Realistic, culturally familiar meal plans
  • Ongoing check-ins and adjustments
  • Collaboration with your doctor or therapist when helpful

FAQs

Questions about nutrition care

Will I have to give up my favourite foods?
No. We focus on balance, portions and smart swaps — not banning foods you love.
Do you work with people who have medical conditions?
Yes. We support diabetes, hypertension, digestive disorders, PCOS and more, and can coordinate with your doctor.
Can nutrition sessions be done online?
Absolutely. Online sessions work very well for nutrition. You may be asked to keep a simple food diary before your appointment.
Do you see children?
Yes. We support children and teenagers with a parent or guardian present.
