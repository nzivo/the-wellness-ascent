FAQs

Seychelles — common questions

Are in-person sessions available in Seychelles?
Not at the moment. All Seychelles services are currently virtual. We will announce any future in-person availability here.
What time zone are appointments in?
Appointments are shown and confirmed in Seychelles time (UTC+4), one hour ahead of Kenya.
Can I have sessions in French?
Yes. Select French as your preferred language when booking and we will match you with a French-speaking practitioner where available.
Can a psychiatrist prescribe medication online?
Where clinically appropriate and permitted by local regulations. In some cases your psychiatrist may coordinate with a local doctor or pharmacy in Seychelles.
What happens in an emergency?
Online sessions are not suitable for emergencies. In Seychelles, call 999 or go to the nearest hospital, such as Seychelles Hospital in Victoria.
