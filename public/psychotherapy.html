What is psychotherapy?

Understanding your story, changing your path

Psychotherapy is a collaborative process between you and a trained professional. Through conversation, reflection and practical tools, it helps you make sense of difficult feelings and patterns.

You do not need to be in crisis to benefit from therapy. Many people come because they feel stuck, overwhelmed, disconnected or simply want to understand themselves better. Our therapists are warm, qualified and trained in evidence-based approaches.

  • Sessions last 50–60 minutes
  • Available in person in Nairobi or online in Kenya and Seychelles
  • English and French-speaking support
  • Strictly confidential

FAQs

Questions about therapy

How many sessions will I need?
It depends on your goals. Some people feel better after 6–8 sessions; others prefer longer-term support. Your therapist will review progress with you regularly.
Is online therapy as effective as in-person?
For many concerns, yes. Research shows virtual therapy can be just as effective. Choose whatever feels most comfortable and practical for you.
Can I bring my partner or family member?
Yes. Couples and family sessions are available. Please select the relevant option when booking or mention it in your notes.
Do you work with teenagers?
Yes, we support adolescents through individual and family sessions. A parent or guardian must give consent for clients under 18.
What if I don’t connect with my therapist?
That is completely okay. A good fit matters. Let us know and we will help you find a practitioner who suits you better.
