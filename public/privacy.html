Privacy policy

Who we are

The Wellness Ascent provides psychotherapy, psychiatric and nutrition services in Kenya and Seychelles. We are the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide: name, email, phone number and country
  • Appointment details: service, session type, preferred dates and any notes you choose to share
  • Messages and testimonials you submit
  • Basic technical data needed to keep the site secure, such as IP address for rate limiting

How we use it

  • To respond to enquiries and manage appointments
  • To send confirmations and reminders
  • To protect the website against misuse

We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

How we protect it

All data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Stored records are encrypted, access is limited to authorised staff, and administrative actions are logged.

Retention

Enquiries are kept for up to 12 months. Clinical records are kept for the period required by professional and legal regulations.

Terms & conditions

  • Information on this website is for general education and is not a substitute for professional advice.
  • This website and our online sessions are not emergency services.
  • Appointment requests are confirmed only once you receive confirmation from our team.
  • Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to cancel or reschedule.
  • Fees shown are indicative and may change.

Cookie policy

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores only small preferences — your language, text size and whether you have seen the cookie notice — on your own device. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.
