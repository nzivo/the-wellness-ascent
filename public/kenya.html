FAQs

Kenya — common questions

Do you accept insurance?
Some insurers cover mental health and nutrition services. We can provide receipts and reports for reimbursement — please check your cover with your provider.
Can I switch between physical and virtual sessions?
Yes. Many clients mix both depending on their schedule. Just let us know when booking.
How soon can I be seen?
We usually offer appointments within a few days. If your need is urgent, please call us directly.
Do you offer corporate or school programmes?
Yes. We provide wellbeing workshops and employee support. Please contact us for a tailored proposal.
