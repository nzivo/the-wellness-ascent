Who we are

One practice. One philosophy. Whole-person care.

The Wellness Ascent is a holistic wellness practice that brings psychotherapy, psychiatry and nutrition together — because the mind, body and daily life are deeply connected.

Like the tree in our logo, lasting wellbeing starts beneath the surface. We help you understand your roots — your story, experiences and patterns — so you can grow steadily toward hope, balance and purpose.

  • Evidence-based care from qualified, licensed practitioners
  • Personalised plans that respect your culture, values and pace
  • Sessions in person in Nairobi or securely online
